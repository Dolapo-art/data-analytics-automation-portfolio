How to read this

Recall measures how many actual churners were identified. Precision measures how many flagged customers really churned. Average precision summarises the precision–recall curve; its baseline is the churn prevalence. Model metrics do not change when cohort filters change.

*Charges are the source dataset’s units, not Nigerian naira. The dashboard describes an observed customer snapshot. Scores classify existing churn labels and are not a validated future churn forecast. Segment differences do not prove causation or demonstrate retention impact. The new evaluation does not substantiate the historical CV’s reported 85% accuracy.
